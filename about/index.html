About FirearmSafety.us

FirearmSafety.us is an independent guide for adults who are new to firearm safety and self-defense training.

We focus on safety, choosing qualified instructors and following the law. We do not sell firearms, ammunition or accessories, and we do not publish tactical instruction. For legal requirements we link to official sources instead of interpreting them.
