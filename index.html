Common questions

Do I need experience to take a firearm safety class?

No. Beginner classes assume no experience. They start with the safety rules and how a firearm works before any live fire.

What should I bring to my first class?

A government-issued photo ID, eye and ear protection unless provided, water, and closed-toe shoes with a crew-neck top.

Do I need a Firearm Safety Certificate in California?

Generally yes, before you can buy a firearm. It is a 30-question test and you need 23 correct to pass.
